
Building an MVP (Minimum Viable Product) for an internal freight dispatching platform means focusing strictly on operational efficiency—eliminating manual spreadsheet updates, centralizing load tracking, and giving dispatchers a single source of truth.Here is a blueprint for a streamlined, functional internal dispatching system.1. Core Feature ModulesFor an internal MVP, skip public job boards or complex automated bidding. Focus purely on execution:🚛 Load ManagementLoad Creation: Quick form to input Pickup/Delivery locations, dates/times, weight, rate, and commodity type.Status Workflow: Standardized load states:Draft $\rightarrow$ Assigned $\rightarrow$ Dispatched $\rightarrow$ In Transit $\rightarrow$ Delivered $\rightarrow$ BilledCustomer Mapping: Link loads to existing internal or external customer accounts.📋 Dispatch BoardKanban / Drag-and-Drop View: Visual board showing loads categorized by status.Driver / Vehicle Assignment: Assign an internal driver and truck/trailer combo to an active load with one click.Conflict Checking: Basic validation to prevent double-booking a driver or vehicle on overlapping time windows.👥 Driver & Asset RosterDriver Profiles: Contact details, CDL numbers, license expiration tracking, and current location/status (Available, On Duty, Off Duty).Equipment List: Truck/Trailer IDs, specs (Reefer, Flatbed, Dry Van), and maintenance alerts.📄 Document & Billing TrackingDocument Attachment: Upload Proof of Delivery (POD), Bill of Lading (BOL), and rate confirmations directly to the load record.Basic Ledger: Record driver pay and load payout status for easy export to accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks).2. Recommended Tech Stack for Quick Internal DeploymentFrontend: React / Next.js (using UI libraries like Shadcn/UI or Mantine for pre-built tables and forms).Backend: Node.js (Express/Fastify) or Python (FastAPI/Django).Database: PostgreSQL (using Prisma or Supabase for rapid backend setup).Low-Code Alternative: If you need this running in days without full custom development, building this on Retool or Appsmith connected to a Supabase database can reduce build time by 80%.
